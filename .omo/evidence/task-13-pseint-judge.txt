## Band boundaries (SPEC §(k))
- OK: ratio < 1.5
- ALTA: 1.5 ≤ ratio < 4.0
- EXCESIVA: ratio ≥ 4.0
- Boundary tests: ratio 1.5 → ALTA (not OK); ratio 4.0 → EXCESIVA (not ALTA).
