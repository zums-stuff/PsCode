## Pinned Ambiguities (documented in issues.md/learnings.md)

1. **Literal operator-step rule**: `a + b * c` = 2 operator steps (SPEC §(g)
   example says 3; corpus pins the literal rule). Case: `step_operators`
   (steps=7: 3 assignments + Escribir stmt + arg + 2 operators).
2. **Para step model**: entry = 1 (statement) + per-iteration = 1 (check) +
   body + 1 (increment) + final failing check = 1. Cases: `para_basico`
   (steps=18), `para_con_paso` (steps=14), `step_para_loop` (steps=14).
3. **Uninitialized read -> ERR_TYPE** (strict, no zero-init). Case:
   `neg_type_error`.
4. **AZAR(100) seed-0 = 49,97,53,5,33**. Case: `azar_pinned`.
5. **RC(1) seed-0 = 'm'**. Case: `rc_fecha_hora`.
6. **SUBCADENA 1-indexed inclusive**: SUBCADENA("abcdef", 2, 4) = "bcd".
   Case: `subcadena_concat`.
7. **REDON half-away-from-zero**: REDON(3.5) = 4, REDON(2.4) = 2.
   Case: `trunc_redon_largo`.
8. **FechaActual()="2026-01-01", HoraActual()="12:00:00"**. Case:
   `rc_fecha_hora`.
