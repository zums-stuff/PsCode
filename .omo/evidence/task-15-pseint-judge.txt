## Design notes
- `Limits` is a frozen dataclass; `effective_limits` uses `dataclasses.replace` over only the `Limits` fields present in the problem dict — unknown keys are ignored by construction.
- No randomization anywhere: no time-based seeds, no random defaults; the module is pure constants (SPEC §(h) determinism).
- No SSE fallback added (plan MUST NOT).
- Engine files untouched; corpus programs read verbatim from `engine/tests/corpus/` (no new corpus cases — TDD-lock domain).
